What an ice dam actually is

The ridge is warm

Heat escaping from the living space warms the upper roof deck. Snow sitting on it melts, even when the air outside is well below freezing.

The eave is cold

The overhang has no warm room beneath it. Meltwater running down from the ridge reaches that cold edge and refreezes, building a ridge of ice along the gutter line.

The water backs up

Behind the dam, water pools and works its way under the shingles — which are built to shed water running down, not water sitting still. That is when it reaches the decking and the ceiling below.

What we can help with, and what we cannot

We can help with

Blocked, sagging, undersized or badly pitched gutters and downspouts that hold standing water going into a freeze. Debris that traps water. Damaged fascia and soffit. Drainage that dumps meltwater where it refreezes.

We will be straight with you about

Ice dams are often driven by attic insulation and ventilation — the warm roof at the top of this page. That is not gutter work. If that is what we find, we will say so and tell you what kind of contractor you actually need, even though it is not a job for us.

No gutter company can promise you will never get an ice dam. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling you something. What good gutters do is stop your drainage being the cause of one, and give meltwater somewhere to go.
